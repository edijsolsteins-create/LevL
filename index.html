LevL

Compare ground heights with two phones.

How to use it

  1. Fix each phone upright to a stake or batten (tape or a rubber band), so its height above the foot of the stake cannot change.
  2. Stand the reader stake at one spot where it can see the whole area. Do not move it while measuring.
  3. Put the target stake on your reference point and tap Set reference.
  4. Move the target stake to any other point. The reader shows how much higher or lower that ground is.

Do the one-time calibration in Setup first. Without it the reading drifts with distance.

Setup

1. Marker size

Measure one side of the black square on the target with a ruler.

2. Distance scale

Place the reader's camera lens a known distance from the marker, facing it squarely, then tap Calibrate.

3. Level calibration

Removes the tilt error of this phone's sensor and camera. Keep both phones on their stakes.

A. Stand both stakes on the same flat surface, 30–50 cm apart.
B. Move the target 3–5 m away. Mark both spots.
C. Swap: reader on the target's spot, target on the reader's spot.

Design level

The height you want relative to the reference point. The reader then shows cut or fill.

Camera resolution

Higher resolution reads the marker from further away but is slower. Repeat steps 2 and 3 after changing it.
